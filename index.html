    About this map

    Green shading marks land between your lowest and highest altitudes. Heights come from global elevation data with about 30 m detail, so a single spot can differ from a local sign by a few tens of metres, more on steep slopes.

    Altitude is only a rough guide to temperature. The same height is warmer nearer the equator and varies with the season.
